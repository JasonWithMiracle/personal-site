A design system I built from scratch for an 8-person product team. We broke common buttons, forms, cards, and modals into reusable components, paired with clear usage rules and accessibility requirements.

After rollout, new page assembly time dropped about 40%, and rework between design and engineering fell noticeably. I've come to believe a design system's value lies not in how much it has, but in whether people actually want to use it.
