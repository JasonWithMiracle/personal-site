Led the reading-end revamp of a long-content platform. We ran many typography experiments and finally settled on a 680px body width, 1.75 line-height, and a separately tuned dark-mode contrast.

After launch, average reading time per user rose 18%. Great reading experiences often hide in details you're not supposed to notice.
