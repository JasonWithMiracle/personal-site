I always wanted a little corner of my own, but didn't want to be tied down by servers, databases, and plugin updates. After trying a few things, I landed on **Astro**.

## Why Astro

- Ships almost no JavaScript by default, so it loads fast;
- Posts are Markdown, which fits my note-taking habit perfectly;
- Free hosting on Netlify, live the moment you push.

## How I update day to day

I write posts right in Obsidian, drop them into the `articles` folder, then publish with a click in GitHub Desktop. Not a single command typed.

For someone who dislikes ops, this is about the most peaceful setup there is.
