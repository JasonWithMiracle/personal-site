Rounding up books I've finished or am reading, with a few quick notes.

## *Thinking, Fast and Slow*

Kahneman splits the mind into two systems and explains why we're easily led by intuition. Building products, remember: most users run on System 1 — don't test their patience.

## *The Design of Everyday Things*

A classic. What struck me most is affordance and feedback — good design tells you what it can do, and tells you what it's doing.

## A small takeaway

Reading slowly is fine; reading *in* is what matters. Better to truly think through three books than skim fifty.
